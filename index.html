Umbrella

Small games about number and shape, built on a simple idea: children learn by doing — collecting, steering, fitting — not by being told. No scores, no timers, no adverts. Pick an activity below. If you want to know the 'why' choose Rationale from the

Tilt Snake
Steer the snake to collect the twelve multiples of a times table, in order. Each one joins its tail.
Snake Puzzle
The snake carries a whole table on its back — but a few numbers are missing. Drag each spawned number home.
Patterns
Find the twelve multiples of a table on the hundred square — watch its pattern emerge as you tap.
Pattern Snake
The snake sheds its twelve segments onto the hundred square, floats up, then waits below for you to place its numbers home.
Lattice
Loose multiples wait on the ground — carry each home to its socket on the hundred square.
Tilt Ball
Steer the ball into a hole that divides it — bar billiards with factors.
Honeycomb Search
A table's multiples hide in the honeycomb as a connected path — pick two tables and they share one cell.
