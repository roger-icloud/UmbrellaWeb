Why this app?

Using fingers brings the learning closer than a mouse or keyboard.

Using a hand held device brings the learning closer to you.

...and not to forget the 'how'

Adopting common interface gestures makes the app more accessible and intuitive.

Rote learning is important and best delivered with personal encouragement. I think a broader understanding of the concepts is a natural pairing.

Not included

No ticks or crosses, red or green — learning is through guidance not negativity.

No league tables or rankings.

No gold coins, rosettes, or other trophies to collect.

No optional purchases

No advertising

No tracking

No data collection

No social media integration

No external links

There will be the opportunity to share progress with family and friends. So that the app remembers where you're at. Always optional and plenty of advance notice before it arrives.
