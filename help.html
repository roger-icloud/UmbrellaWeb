The activities

Tilt Snake — steer the snake to a table's multiples, in order. Tilt the device or drag a finger.

Snake Puzzle — a snake shows a table with four gaps. Drag each missing number home.

Snake Patterns — the snake lays a table on the hundred square, then takes four numbers away. Put them back.

Tilt Ball — roll each numbered ball into a hole whose number divides it. Tilt, drag, or tap a hole. Any hole that fits is right.

Find Tables — a table's multiples hide in the honeycomb as a path, each touching the last. Choose two or three tables and the paths can cross.

For adults

Children learn by doing, not by being told. No scores, timers, adverts, accounts or cookies. Anonymous play statistics may be collected to improve the activities.

Add to your home screen

Best installed like an app. Safari (iPad, iPhone): Share, then "Add to Home Screen". Chrome (Android): ⋮ menu, then "Install app". It then opens full-screen.
