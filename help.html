The activities

Tilt Snake — tilt the device (or drag a finger) to steer the snake to the twelve multiples of a chosen times table, in order. Each catch joins its tail.

Patterns — find the twelve multiples on the hundred square, in any order, and watch the table's pattern appear.

Honeycomb Search — a table's multiples hide in the honeycomb as a connected path: each next answer touches the one you just found. Pick two or three tables and their paths may share cells.

Tilt Ball — a bare probe: how does tilting the device feel to steer? Nothing to win.

For adults

Children learn by doing — collecting, steering, fitting — not by being told. No scores, no timers, no adverts. Anonymous play statistics may be collected to improve the activities.

Add to your home screen

The games work best installed like an app. In Safari (iPhone/iPad): tap the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen". In Chrome (Android): tap the ⋮ menu, then "Add to Home screen" or "Install app". The snake icon then opens the activity full-screen, with no browser buttons.

Feedback

Adults: comments, corrections and classroom reports are welcome via the Feedback option in the menu (snake icon, top right). No accounts, no cookies, no adverts.
