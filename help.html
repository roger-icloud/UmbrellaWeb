The activities

Tilt Snake — steer the snake to a table's multiples, in order. Tilt the device or drag a finger.

Snake Puzzle — a snake shows a table with four gaps. Drag each missing number home.

Honeycomb — A hexagon with intersecting lines shows a table's multiples. Find them all. A long touch or mouse click is revealing!

Tables Twist — a table's multiples hide, in the grid as a path, each touching the last. Choose two or three tables and the paths can cross. They can be twisty.

Tilt Ball — roll each numbered ball into a hole whose number divides it. Tilt, drag, or tap a hole. Any hole that fits is right.

Patterns — each table has a pattern on a 100 square Try adding a table or two to see the patterns and where they share a number.

For adults

Children learn by doing, not by being told. Little or no instruction, no scores, timers, adverts, accounts or cookies. Anonymous play statistics may be collected to improve the activities.

Add to your home screen

Best installed like an app. Safari (iPad, iPhone): Share, then "Add to Home Screen". Chrome (Android): ⋮ menu, then "Install app". It then opens full-screen.
