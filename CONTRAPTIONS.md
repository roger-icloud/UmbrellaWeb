# CONTRAPTIONS — the mechanical universe

Design embryo, distilled from the overnight discussion (2026-11-20, ~03:00).
One page. Everything here is up for argument; nothing is built.

## The idea

A world of small machines — flagpole was house zero. Pieces have
physical properties (size, weight, wholeness); arithmetic operations
change those properties; the machine sorts, lifts, tips, rolls pieces
by what they now measure. Number as a physical fact, not a label.

Influences: TIM (routed energy/matter, Heath Robinson chains), the
pull-the-pin genre (single-screen cross-section, sequence as the
puzzle — but our pins and gates are *arithmetic*).

## The spine (what flagpole generalised)

A contraption is four things: a shelf of parts, an `anchors()` map,
a `running()` rule ("the machine is complete"), and a draw loop.
Interaction grammar settled and shared: drag, glowing anchor while
carrying, amber refusal on the wrong anchor, glide-home on a miss.

## House rules argued tonight

- **One dialect per level.** This level speaks fractions, that one
  magnitude — never three arithmetics on one screen. Variety lives
  *between* levels. (Cabinet-of-curiosities risk.)
- **Spectacle is the reward, gated on finding *a* correct path —
  never the one right answer.** Several right routes seeded in.
- **Refusals are physical, not penal.** Wrong coin reaches the gate,
  won't fit, bounces, trundles home. Same law as the amber flash,
  enacted.
- **Near-miss beats refusal.** A wrong total lands at 998 and shows
  it — the pull to try again lives in the almost.
- **Nothing is arbitrary.** "Inflate with enough odd numbers" was
  struck for this: the child can't see why odd. Pie-chart inflation
  survives — the wheel is literally not whole until the fractions
  fill it; the circle *is* the sum.

## Goals weighed (appeal × mathematics)

- **Journey/delivery** — get the flag to the summit, the coin to its
  slot. Numbers are passports; richest vein.
- **Balance/equivalence** — pure arithmetic, needs dressing.
- **Rescue** (pull-pin) — highest appeal, hardest to mathematise.
- **Accumulation** — the lamp needs N units of weight; coins sum to
  it. Arithmetic as fuel; composes with everything.

Synthesis argued: **each level is a small wish; arithmetic is how it
is granted.** A lamp wants lighting, a door wants opening — the child
thinks they're helping, not calculating.

## The earned-parts loop (the load-bearing idea)

Tables earn parts; parts build the machine. Practice in the tables
games ships a gear, a fan, a pulley into the child's workshop — the
contraption grows over weeks. This is the parked "construction
rewards" axis (AGENTS.md: flower +petal, machine +piece) finally
finding its purpose — success tangible *and usable*, never counted.

## First patch — the coin sorter (recommended)

Why the sorter over the tyre pump: it has the selection task
(choose coins from stock under constraint — our "choosing, not
counting" grammar), and its level-descend mechanism *is* the road
between houses — the topology question answered by the first level.
The tyre pump is house two: it fuses transform + fit + journey in
one wheel but needs the fraction dialect settled.

The sketch: five avenues below a ramp. A stock of coins, values
shown. Each avenue's path is governed by a criterion. Drag a coin
to an avenue's mouth; it *rolls* to its gate — fits, drops home;
wrong, bounces back. One coin per avenue; all five seated → the
shelf descends like a lift, tilts into a ramp feeding the next
sorter below. A rope-and-pulley can counterweight the descent to
raise the next stage — nothing moves for free.

Criteria — hybrid: gate aperture encodes magnitude where it can
(only a coin of diameter 6 fits); a small quiet mark carries what
shape can't (parity, multiple-of). Number-as-dimension where free,
honest marks elsewhere.

## Infrastructure position

Keep flagpole's skeleton + grammar; **no framework until the second
house exists.** Levels should eventually be data (parts, anchors,
completion rule, reward) — the city grows by writing level files,
never re-plumbing. Extract what repeats when we have two instances,
not before.

## The bank of devices (to draw from, not to use all)

- Move: ramp, slope, conveyor, lift, pulley-counterweight, seesaw
- Redirect: gate, pin, deflector, switch, one-way flap
- Transform: inflate/deflate, squash, charge/uncharge (weight)
- Store: tank, hopper, platform (weight accumulates), cord loop
- Trigger: switch, plug-pull, pressure plate, tipping point
- Reveal: light, vacuum, door — "let the user see further"
- Blow/spin: fan, windmill, water wheel
