# Session notes — 2026-11-12

## mix-patterns.html — built today (Roger's homework for Devin)

A large hundred square ringed by satellite mini-squares — one per
table ×2…×12 parked on the four edge strips, numbers reading unbroken
clockwise (2,3,4 top; 5,6,7 right; 8 bottom-right, clear, 9
bottom-left; 10,11,12 up the left). Drag (or tap) a satellite to lay
its pattern; again to lift it. Patterns accumulate — shared cells pay
their colours as a clipped conical blend (hand-rolled wedges, no
createConicGradient — iPhone-8 Safari). Three deep max (`MAX_OVERLAY`)
— a fourth is refused with the amber shake grammar; the drop-zone rim
glows amber while a refused satellite hovers. Clear lives among the
satellites as a two-tone eraser tile. `?tables=3,5` opens with a mix
pre-laid — shareable combinations, may matter for help/rationale.

Choices made: satellites show **all** multiples in the square (the
classic hundred-square texture — comparison needs it; the first-twelve
rule stays on patterns.html). Active tiles wear a pastel lift of their
colour (`PASTEL_LIFT`); idle ones dim (`DIM_ALPHA`) so busy mixes stay
readable. No completion/departure — it is a sandbox, deliberately
outside the den journey.

Still uncommitted when written; awaiting Roger's verdict on feel.

## Design ruling — encouragement vs accolades (parked for V1.2)

Roger's instinct: degree, not binary. Devin's formulation Roger
accepted: **the app may remember; it doesn't keep score for display.**
Encouragement is ephemeral (celebration plays, leaves nothing);
accolades persist for an audience. Three axes — persistence,
addressee, voice (information = safe, statement = scrutinised).
Precedent already public in the intro: "the app remembers where
you're at". Roger endorsed the atlas reading: **the layout persisting
as an atlas that marks where I've been — and perhaps where I've
learned.** Revisit under tracking/adaptive for V1.2.
